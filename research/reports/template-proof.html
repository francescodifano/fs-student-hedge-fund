The Macro Backdrop

The proximate cause of the 2026 inflation impulse is the Iran conflict and the disruption to oil and gas supply. Brent began the year near $66, climbed to a peak around $119 at the end of March, then eased back as peace expectations built. The energy shock lifted headline inflation everywhere and fed into core measures with a lag.

Policy divergence

The Federal Reserve is holding while the European Central Bank tightens into weak growth. That divergence anchors all three sleeves: it shapes the dollar path for the equity allocation, the duration stance in fixed income, and the real-asset case in commodities.
