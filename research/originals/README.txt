VIEWING THE COMMENTS

PDFs: comments are attached to the highlighted passages. Hover or click a
highlight to read the note, or open the comments or annotations panel in your
PDF reader for the full list. Where a summary note exists it is the icon on
the title page. Gold highlights are flags, green highlights are credits.

Word documents: open in the Word desktop application rather than the browser
version, otherwise the comments and the layout may not render correctly.
Comments appear in the right margin.

NOTES
